How we work

An owner, not an agent

OHPC Pinellas LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L16000092753, filed in 2016, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We lease only property we own, so we are not real estate brokers and charge no commission to anyone. We are also not attorneys. Commercial tenants in Florida have far fewer statutory protections than residential tenants, and a commercial lease is a negotiated contract rather than a standard form — have yours reviewed by your own attorney before signing, and treat any landlord who discourages that as a warning.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

What is CAM and why does it matter?

Common area maintenance — your share of maintaining parking, lighting, landscaping and shared areas, usually billed on top of rent. A low rent with heavy CAM can cost more than a higher rent without it, which is why we itemise it before you sign.

Do I need my own attorney?

Yes. Commercial leases are negotiated contracts and commercial tenants have few statutory protections in Florida. Any landlord who suggests you do not need one is telling you something.

Can I run my business from this unit?

Check with the local authority before committing. Zoning and permitted use are not ours to guarantee, and discovering a problem after signing is expensive.

Who fixes the roof?

Stated in the lease, and we would rather agree that boundary in writing than argue about it during a storm.

Do you charge a fee to view or apply?

No. We own the units and lease them directly, so there is no agent fee and no commission.

What happens at renewal?

The lease sets out how and when rent escalates. We will talk about renewal well before expiry rather than at the last minute.
