Program 02 · Including the quarterly ones

Lease terms in plain language

A low headline rent with heavy recoverable charges costs more than a higher rent with none, and tenants rarely find that out until the first reconciliation. We set out every recurring sum, state which are estimates, and explain how escalations work before anyone signs.

Program 03 · Trading on the agreed date

Fit-out coordination

Most disputes at the start of a tenancy are about who was doing what by when. We put the split in writing — landlord works, tenant works, who pulls which permit — so a business can plan an opening date rather than discovering a two-month delay.
